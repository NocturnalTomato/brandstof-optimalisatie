# Brandstof

Work out where to buy fuel on a long drive, so you stop as few times as possible and
pay as little as you can.

Enter your route, your car, how full the tank is and how full you want it on arrival.
You get the stations to stop at, how many litres to buy at each, what it costs, and a
Google or Apple Maps route with the stops as waypoints.

**Start with [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md)** — it explains the whole thing: decisions,
architecture, conventions and the two gotchas that will otherwise waste your time.
